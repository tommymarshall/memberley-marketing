## Design Context

### Users

Memberley serves volunteer board members, club operators, front-desk teams, and program leaders at pool, swim-and-tennis, court, and field clubs. They need to run memberships, payments, facilities, classes, events, communications, and day-of operations without stitching together several systems.

### Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and human. Memberley should feel like a thoughtful operational partner: precise enough to earn a board's trust, approachable enough for seasonal staff, and confident without sounding like enterprise software.

### Aesthetic Direction

A warm editorial interface built around the Ember palette: ivory surfaces, restrained peach and lavender atmosphere, dark ink typography, real product imagery, and generous breathing room. The visual references are Stripe's precise navigation and subtle motion, Native's faded hero atmosphere, and Family's narrow, simple composition. Avoid generic SaaS card grids, gratuitous glass effects, synthetic testimonials, and AI-looking product mockups.

### Design Principles

- Lead with the real operational job and make the product proof specific.
- Keep hierarchy editorial and spacious, with asymmetry used for emphasis.
- Treat Ember as the permanent palette and reserve stronger contrast for actions.
- Prefer real screenshots, concrete workflows, and honest comparison language.
- Adapt layouts for mobile without hiding important content or interactions.
